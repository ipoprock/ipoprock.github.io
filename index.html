🚀 What Is bops?

Most software waits for you to click something. bops does not. Each bot in your bops team is a fully capable AI worker with its own environment — a real desktop, its own inbox, and its own phone line. You describe the outcome you want, and the bot figures out the steps, opens the tools it needs, and delivers the result.

Think of it as a small department that never sleeps. One bot answers customer emails overnight. Another follows up on invoices in the morning. A third makes the phone call your team keeps postponing. They all run in the background on your own computer, and you can check in on any of them at any time to see exactly what they are doing.

A note on privacy: bops is open source and built to run on your machine. You control which bots exist, which accounts they can reach, and when they are allowed to act.

✨ Key Features

💻 Every Bot Gets Its Own Computer

Each AI teammate has a separate desktop session with its own browser, files, and applications. It can open websites, fill in forms, download documents, and save files — just like a person sitting at a keyboard. Because each bot has its own computer, two bots can work at the same time without stepping on each other's toes.

📧 A Real Email Address per Bot

Give a bot an inbox and it can read incoming messages, sort what matters, draft replies in your voice, and escalate the tricky ones to you. Your customers get a response in minutes instead of days, and you never wonder whether an important message slipped through the cracks.

📞 A Phone Number That Makes Calls

Some jobs still need a phone call — confirming an appointment, checking on a delivery, chasing a supplier. A bops bot can place that call, hold a natural conversation, and write up a summary of what was said afterwards, so you have a record without lifting the handset.

🧩 A Team, Not a Single Tool

You can build a team of specialists: one for billing, one for scheduling, one for onboarding new clients. Each one has its own identity, its own tools, and its own set of instructions. You are the manager — you set the goals and review the work.

🖥️ A Simple Desktop App

bops installs like any normal Windows program. There are no servers to configure and no commands to type. Open the application, watch your bots work in their own windows, and pause them whenever you like.

🔓 Open Source and Yours to Inspect

The whole project is open source. Anyone can read the code, suggest improvements, or adapt it to their own business. That transparency matters when software is acting on your behalf.

🧭 Your First Bot in Five Steps

Once the application is open, you do not need to know anything about programming to get value out of it. The setup is written in plain language and asks you simple questions.

  1. Name your bot. Something human and memorable helps — "Riley from Accounts" is easier to think about than "Bot 3".
  2. Describe the job in everyday words. For example: "Every weekday morning, check the support inbox, reply to simple questions, and flag anything about refunds."
  3. Connect the tools it needs. You choose which email account and which phone number the bot may use. Nothing is connected until you say so.
  4. Watch the first run. The bot shows its work on its own screen. Follow along, and correct it if it heads in the wrong direction — it learns from the correction.
  5. Let it run. Once you are happy with the result, leave it working. You can pause, edit, or delete any bot whenever you like.

💡 Who bops Is For

bops suits small business owners, freelancers, solo founders, and busy operations managers who spend too much of the day on repetitive admin. If your to-do list is full of small tasks that follow the same pattern every time — replying to similar emails, chasing invoices, confirming bookings, updating spreadsheets — a bot can take that job off your hands.

It is also a good fit for teams who want to experiment with AI assistants in a controlled way. Because every bot works on its own computer with its own accounts, you can give one a narrow, well-defined job and see how it performs before expanding its responsibilities.

🛠️ How It Works Under the Hood

bops is built as a desktop application using Electron, with a modern Next.js interface. That combination means it feels like a normal Windows program but is built with the same technology that powers many web applications. The computer-use layer lets each bot operate a genuine desktop environment rather than pretending to click buttons in a script.

For the technically curious, the repository contains the full source, setup notes, and issue tracker. For everyone else, none of that matters — you interact with bops through a simple window, not a terminal.

🔐 Keeping Things Under Control

Giving software a computer and a phone number is a big step, so bops is designed to keep you in charge. You decide which accounts a bot can touch, you can watch its screen at any moment, and you can stop it instantly. Bots act only within the instructions you have written for them, and every action it takes is visible on its own desktop.

A sensible approach is to start small. Give a new bot one narrow job for the first week, review the results each day, and only widen its responsibilities once you are comfortable with how it behaves. That habit gives you the benefits of automation without any unwelcome surprises.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. bops is designed for people who run a business, not people who write software. Everything is configured through the application window using ordinary language.

How many bots can I run?

You can create as many bots as your computer can comfortably handle. Most people start with one or two and add more as they find tasks worth handing over.

What happens if a bot gets something wrong?

You can review its work, correct it, and pause it at any time. Because each bot shows its activity on its own screen, mistakes are easy to spot and easy to fix.
